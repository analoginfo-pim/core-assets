## What this is

An original curated list of applications operators commonly place under
endpoint privilege / application elevation controls (shells, scripting hosts,
installers, admin tools, browsers, office). Paths and publisher subjects are
illustrative starting points — operators must verify against their signed
binaries before Enforce.
