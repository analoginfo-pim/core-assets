# Research pointers (citation only)

This folder holds attribution notes for cite-only pointers that are **not**
bundled into the product catalog. Do not place third-party article prose,
scraped tables, or vendor blog URLs here unless Phil explicitly adds a
commercially redistributable CSV/JSON with a clear SPDX license.

Bundled credential data lives under sibling folders (`defaultcreds`,
`scadapass`, `seclists-default`, `cisa-ics`) and is listed in
`../sources-manifest.json`.
